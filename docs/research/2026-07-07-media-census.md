# Media census + adversarial schema stress-test — 2026-07-07 (evidence for `knowledge-ontology.md` v3)

> 7 Fable agents (`wf_cb28d99e-c60`, 7/7): 4 census tracks enumerated every major platform's content types (2026-current, live-web-verified) incl. non-platform cases (web pages, bare links, local files), distilled to **32 structural archetypes**; 3 adversarial fit-test tracks then instantiated each archetype in the v2 schema and tried to break it. **Result: 45 gaps (14 blocking / 25 important / 6 minor), all folded into v3.** Ordered by the founder's directive: test categorically, every media type, every platform, before data is stored.


## Part 1 — the census


### Census domain 1: Video & audio platforms

**TikTok** — Feed video (short-form up to 10 min GA; 60-min pilot for ~1,200 creators since Jan 2026); Photo carousel / slideshow (2-35 images, one permalink, background music overlay); Stories (24h ephemeral, 60s video or image); LIVE (real-time broadcast, gifting; limited replay); Series (paywalled collection: up to 80 videos x 20 min, $0.99-$189.99 one-time purchase); Playlists (creator-curated groupings on profile); Sound/audio pages (a sound is a first-class linkable entity aggregating all videos using it); Duets & Stitches (derivative posts referencing a parent video)
  
*Structural:* Carousel = N images under ONE permalink with a music track applied to the whole container · Paywall at the collection level (Series), not per-item · 24h ephemerality (Stories) alongside permanent feed · Derivative links: duet/stitch items carry a structural pointer to a parent video · Video length now spans 3 seconds to 60 minutes on the same platform — duration is not a platform-level constant

**Instagram** — Reels (vertical video, up to 3 min; every video post is auto-shared as a Reel); Trial Reels (variant: shown to non-followers only, hidden from grid unless promoted); Single image post; Carousel (2-20 mixed photos/videos, aspect ratio locked by slide 1, per-video slide <=60s); Feed video (legacy IGTV merged in; 10 min standard, 60 min for larger accounts); Stories (24h, 60s/segment, interactive stickers/polls/links; persist only via Highlights); Highlights (curated permanent Story collections on profile); Notes (<=60-char text/emoji, 72h, lives in DM inbox surface, mutuals-only); Live (up to 4h, co-hosts, saveable/convertible to Reel after); Broadcast channels (one-to-many DM channel: text, voice, photo, video, polls; 1.5B msgs/month, expanded 2025-26)
  
*Structural:* Carousel: one permalink, up to 20 heterogeneous sub-items (photo+video mixed) · Trial Reels: same media, different DISTRIBUTION state — visibility is a mutable property of the item · Notes and Broadcast-channel messages have no conventional web permalink · Stories → Highlights: ephemeral item can be promoted to permanent under a new container · Guides were deprecated (late 2023) yet still appear in 2026 SEO guides — third-party 'format lists' contain zombie types

**YouTube** — Long-form video (16:9, chapters, timestamps, transcripts); Shorts (vertical, up to 3 min since Oct 2024); Live streams (DVR; become VODs on the channel); Premieres (scheduled watch-party debut of an uploaded video: hybrid on-demand/live); Community/'Posts' (text, up to 10 images, polls, quizzes; surfaces in Home/Shorts feeds since Jan 2025 Communities Hub); Playlists (incl. Podcasts — a podcast IS a playlist type — and Courses, optionally paywalled); Members-only videos/posts (channel-level paywall); Viewer Clips (5-60s pointer into a parent video — SUNSET: replaced by 'Share at Timestamp' from Apr 16, 2026; existing clips remain viewable); Hype (fan-boost mechanic for videos from 500-500K-sub channels, launched early 2026)
  
*Structural:* Podcasts and Courses are not new media types — they are TYPED PLAYLISTS over ordinary videos · Premieres: an on-demand asset that passes through a scheduled live phase with chat · Clips were pure time-range POINTERS (no re-encode) — and the format died in 2026; timestamp-in-URL replaced it · Community posts are text/interactive units living inside a video platform, with their own permalinks (youtube.com/post/...) · Same video can be public, unlisted, members-only, or scheduled — access state is orthogonal to media type

**Snapchat** — Snaps (view-once/twice ephemeral photo-video messages); Stories (24h; Friends-only or Public); Public Profile saved Stories (creators can persist public Stories to profile permanently); Spotlight (permanent, algorithmic short-form vertical video feed — TikTok-class); Memories (private cloud archive of saved snaps — user-only visibility); Snap Map / location stories (geo-aggregated ephemeral content)
  
*Structural:* Ephemerality is the DEFAULT; permanence (Spotlight, saved public Stories, Memories) is the exception · Memories = a private saved-content archive INSIDE the platform — direct prior art for Commonplace capture · Most Snap content has no public permalink at all; capture must happen at view time · Same clip can exist simultaneously as ephemeral Story and permanent Spotlight post

**Twitch** — Live streams (primary format; chat, bits, raids); VODs / past broadcasts (full stream recordings — EXPIRE: 7 days basic, 14 Affiliate, 60 Partner/Turbo/Prime); Highlights (streamer-marked permanent VOD segments; capped at 100 hours/channel since May 19, 2025); Clips (viewer-created <=60s standalone re-encodes; never expire); Stories (48h ephemeral posts, mobile-only, monetized streamers — still active per Twitch help docs); Subscriber-only VODs (paywalled archive access)
  
*Structural:* ARCHIVE DECAY is core: the canonical recording self-deletes on a 7/14/60-day timer — a saved Twitch VOD link WILL die · Three-tier derivation chain: live → VOD (expiring) → highlight/clip (permanent) — permanence requires explicit extraction · Clips are standalone re-encoded assets (survive parent deletion); contrast with YouTube's pointer-style clips (didn't) · Paywall on the ARCHIVE (sub-only VODs) while the live event was free

**Spotify** — Music tracks / albums / singles; Playlists (user, collaborative, Blend, editorial, algorithmic); Podcast shows & episodes — audio AND video (500M+ users have streamed video podcasts); Audiobooks (700K+ titles; paywalled via metered listening-hours on Premium); Narrated Articles (long-form magazine articles as audio — test launched May 26, 2026, 650+ titles); Personal Podcasts (AI-GENERATED private episodes tailored to the listener — no public identity at all); Clips (<30s vertical videos on artist/release pages — FROZEN: no new uploads after June 17, 2026; existing remain); Full-length artist videos (music videos, live performances, studio sessions — royalty-bearing, replaced Clips June 2026)
  
*Structural:* Same episode can have dual audio+video representations under one canonical ID · Audiobook paywall is metered by listening HOURS, not per-item purchase · Personal Podcasts: content generated per-user that exists for exactly one person — no shareable canonical URL · Playback position is first-class user state (episodes/audiobooks) · Narrated Articles: text journalism re-mediated as audio — cross-media derivation as a platform format

**Apple Podcasts** — Shows (RSS-backed or Apple-hosted); Episodes (audio; video via HLS announced Feb 2026 with watch/listen toggle and offline video download); Channels (publisher-level grouping of shows); Premium subscriptions (paywalled shows, episodes, and channels; monthly/annual); Auto-generated transcripts (125M+ episodes, 13 languages); Auto-generated chapters (from transcripts, English); Timed links (timestamped in-playback banner links)
  
*Structural:* Three-level hierarchy: channel → show → episode · Open-RSS and closed-paywalled content coexist in one catalog — same 'episode' type, different retrievability · Transcript + chapter metadata is now platform-generated, not creator-supplied — rich nav metadata exists even when the creator provided none · Seamless audio↔video switching on a single episode entity (2026)

**SoundCloud** — Tracks (songs, demos, remixes, DJ mixes, podcast audio, spoken word — one 'track' type spans all); Playlists & albums (an album is a typed playlist); Reposts (share-to-followers reference, core feed mechanic); Waveform comments (comments anchored to a timestamp on the audio waveform); Private-link tracks (unlisted, token-URL sharing); Stations / algorithmic playlists
  
*Structural:* Hosts long-form continuous audio (multi-hour DJ mixes, ~58K new mixes/week) that can't exist on licensed DSPs · Timestamp-anchored social annotation (waveform comments) — annotations are positioned IN the media timeline · Repost = a reference-type item in feeds (save-worthy items may be pointers, not assets) · Token-URL private content — retrievability depends on possessing the secret link

**Sora (OpenAI) — newcomer, already dead** — AI-generated vertical short videos (~10s-1min, generated in-app); Cameos (consented likeness insertion into others' generations); Remixes (derivative re-generations of existing posts)
  
*Structural:* Launched Sept 30, 2025; 1M+ downloads in <5 days; DISCONTINUED March 24, 2026 — an entire platform's permalinks died within 6 months · 100% AI-generated content: provenance/generation metadata (prompt, model, cameo consent) is the substance · Remix chains: every item potentially derivative of another

**Meta Vibes — newcomer** — AI-generated short-form videos (feed inside Meta AI app; EU rollout Nov 2025; standalone app under consideration per PYMNTS 2026); Remixed AI videos (re-prompt someone else's generation)
  
*Structural:* All-AI feed — no human-captured media at all · Lives inside another app (Meta AI) — canonical URL scheme unstable while the host app shifts

**Loops / Skylight / Neptune — federated newcomers** — Loops (ActivityPub/Pixelfed): short vertical videos, duets; iOS launch Jan 30, 2026; ~31 servers, 40.8K accounts; Skylight (AT Protocol): TikTok-style short video; likes/follows mirror to Bluesky; hit #2 US App Store Entertainment; Neptune (AT Protocol): community-oriented short video, early development
  
*Structural:* Federated identity: one logical item may exist across many servers/instances with different URLs · Cross-protocol mirroring (Skylight engagement mirrors into Bluesky) — the same act produces records on two networks · Small but structurally novel: schema should key on protocol-level IDs (AT-URI, ActivityPub object ID), not just https URLs

**Archetypes distilled:**
- **1. Atomic on-demand AV asset** — covers: TikTok feed video, Instagram Reel / feed video / single image, YouTube video & Short, Snapchat Spotlight, Twitch Clip & Highlight, Spotify track / episode / audiobook / artist video, Apple Podcasts episode, SoundCloud track. Key properties: Stable permalink + platform-native ID (or protocol ID: AT-URI/ActivityPub); Single media timeline with duration; optional chapters, timestamps, transcript; Dual representations possible (audio+video of same episode: Spotify, Apple 2026); May reference a first-class AUDIO ENTITY (TikTok/Reels sound page) — the sound itself is saveable; AI-provenance fields (model, prompt, remix-parent, cameo consent) now required — Sora/Vibes made generation metadata the substance
- **2. Multi-item swipe container (carousel)** — covers: TikTok photo carousel (2-35 images + music overlay), Instagram carousel (2-20 mixed photo/video slides), YouTube community image posts (<=10 images). Key properties: ONE permalink, N ordered sub-items — index position is semantic (step 3 of a tutorial); Sub-items are heterogeneous (photo + video mixed in one Instagram carousel); Container-level media overlay (TikTok music applies to the whole carousel, not any slide); Schema needs: parent item + ordered child media array, each child with own mime/dimensions/duration
- **3. Curated collection / series (list-of-references)** — covers: YouTube playlists / Podcasts / Courses, Spotify playlists & albums & shows, Apple Podcasts shows & channels, TikTok playlists & Series, SoundCloud playlists/albums, Instagram Highlights. Key properties: Container REFERENCES items that also exist independently — save the membership+order, not copies; Membership mutates over time (playlists churn); a snapshot-at-save-time is part of the capture; Container carries its own metadata AND its own paywall (TikTok Series $0.99-189.99; YouTube Courses; Apple premium channels); Platforms type-tag containers (podcast=playlist, album=playlist, course=playlist) — 'collection kind' is an enum, not a new schema; Hierarchies nest 3 deep (Apple: channel → show → episode)
- **4. Ephemeral story-class unit** — covers: Instagram Stories & Notes, TikTok Stories, Snapchat Snaps & Stories, Twitch Stories, Instagram broadcast-channel messages. Key properties: TTL is intrinsic: 24h (IG/TikTok/Snap), 48h (Twitch), 72h (IG Notes) — or view-once (Snaps); Weak or NO web permalink — capture must copy media at view time; a URL reference is worthless after expiry; Sequence-of-segments model (a 'story' is N segments, each with own media + timestamp); Interactive overlays (polls, stickers, links) are part of the content, not decoration; Promotion path to permanence exists (Story→Highlight, Snap→Memories/public profile) — lifecycle state must be mutable
- **5. Live session with decaying archive** — covers: Twitch live→VOD, YouTube live & Premieres, Instagram Live, TikTok LIVE. Key properties: TWO-PHASE lifecycle: live event (chat, unbounded duration, no seekable asset) → optional archive (VOD); ARCHIVE DECAY: Twitch VODs self-delete in 7/14/60 days; IG Live persists only if creator saves; permanence requires explicit extraction (highlight/clip); The archive may be paywalled even when the live event was free (Twitch sub-only VODs); Premieres invert it: pre-recorded asset passes through a scheduled live phase; Schema needs: live/archived/expired state, scheduled-start, archive-expiry-date, extracted-permanent-children links
- **6. Time-range reference into a parent asset** — covers: Twitch Clips (standalone re-encode), YouTube viewer Clips (pure pointer — sunset Apr 2026), Spotify Clips (frozen June 2026), YouTube Share-at-Timestamp, TikTok duets/stitches, SoundCloud waveform comments, Apple timed links & chapters. Key properties: An item defined by (parent_asset_id, t_start, [t_end]) — the pointer is the content; Two implementations with opposite survival: re-encoded standalone (Twitch clips outlive parent) vs pure pointer (YouTube clips died WITH the feature, Apr 2026); This is the most VOLATILE archetype: two platforms killed their pointer format within 3 months in 2026 — store parent-ID + offsets yourself, never rely on the platform's clip object; Timestamped annotations (waveform comments, timed links, chapters) are the same shape: metadata anchored inside a timeline
- **7. Channel-scoped interactive micro-post** — covers: YouTube community Posts (text/images/polls/quizzes), Instagram broadcast channels & Notes, Snapchat public-profile posts. Key properties: Text-first unit attached to an AV creator channel — media platforms all grew a micro-blog; Interactive payloads (poll options + live results, quizzes with answers) — results are time-varying data, capture is a snapshot; Permalink quality varies: YouTube posts have URLs; broadcast-channel messages and Notes effectively don't; Distribution surface is the DM inbox or feed injection, not a profile grid

### Census domain 2: Text & social/discussion platforms

**X / Twitter** — standard post (text ≤280 free / ≤25,000 Premium; up to 4 images, GIF, video ≤2:20 free / ≤4h Premium); self-thread (chained posts, up to ~25); reply (forms conversation tree); quote post (embeds another post + commentary); repost (identity share); poll (2–4 options, 5 min–7 days); long-form post (25k chars w/ rich formatting, Premium); Article (separate rich-editor document, Premium, search-indexed, $1M 2026 contest); Community post (visible only inside an X Community; supports all regular formats); Space (live audio session, optional recording/replay); live video broadcast; DM / X Chat message; List (curated account collection); bookmark + bookmark folders (private saves, folders are Premium); subscriber-only post/Space (creator subscriptions paywall)
  
*Structural:* quote-repost edge graph plus reply trees plus self-threading — three distinct edge types on one node · since March 2025 external-link posts from free accounts get ~0 distribution, so links migrate to first reply — the 'content' and its link are structurally split across two nodes · edit window (1h, Premium) creates version history · Spaces are time-bound with optional replay artifact · paywalled (subscriber-only) variants of ordinary post types

**Reddit** — text post (Markdown body); link post (title+URL; optional body text since 2025 rollout); image post / gallery (multi-image with per-image captions); video post (native v.redd.it player); poll post (system migrated 2025; mod-only segmented polls added Mar 2026); comment (nested scored tree); crosspost (post that embeds another post across subreddits); AMA (convention layered on text post + comment tree); subreddit (community container with per-sub allowed post types); user-profile post; multireddit / custom feed (bundle of subreddits); saved posts & saved comments; upvoted history; post flair / user flair (per-subreddit taxonomy, up to 350 templates)
  
*Structural:* comment forest with multiple server-side sort orders (best/top/new/controversial) and collapse logic · body text now allowed on ALL post types — the text/link/media distinction is a payload variant, not a type · crosspost is a first-class embed edge between communities · flair is a per-community controlled vocabulary attached to posts · removed vs deleted vs archived states; NSFW/spoiler flags gate rendering

**Threads** — text post (≤500 chars); carousel media post (up to 20 mixed images/videos); video post (≤5 min); poll (24h); reply / self-thread; quote post and repost; ghost post (auto-archives after 24h; replies routed to DMs, likes hidden); DM (added 2025); custom feed (user-built, shareable/public, pinnable); community post (topic communities, worldwide beta Oct 2025, 100+ topics); topic tag (one per post, feeds discovery)
  
*Structural:* ghost posts: ephemerality on the SAME node type, with replies structurally rerouted to a different surface (DMs) · communities built on top of topic tags + custom feeds rather than as walled containers · ActivityPub federation — posts can leave the platform · quote controls / reply approvals: per-post permission gates · cross-recommendation into Instagram/Facebook feeds

**Bluesky** — post (300 chars; ≤4 images, GIF, video ≤3 min); reply / thread; quote post and repost; DM / chat; custom feed (algorithmic feed as a first-class addressable object); list (user lists, moderation lists); starter pack (bundle of up to 150 accounts + 3 feeds, own permalink/QR); LIVE badge (profile pointer to external Twitch/Streamplace stream); cashtags ($AAPL) and hashtags (Jan 2026); video tab on profiles
  
*Structural:* every item is an AT Protocol record in the user's own portable repo — the only major network where the data layer itself is export-friendly · NO native polls (third-party embeds only) · feeds/lists/starter packs are saveable container objects with their own URIs · live content is referenced, never hosted (LIVE badge links out) · threadgates: per-post reply-permission records

**Mastodon / fediverse** — status/toot (500 chars default, per-server variable; media attachments); reply; boost (repost); quote post (v4.5, Nov 2025, consent-based per FEP-044f); poll (native ActivityPub Question object); content-warning wrapped status; edited status with visible version history; DM (a status with 'direct' visibility scope — not a separate chat system); bookmark, favourite, list, pinned post; federated foreign objects in-timeline: long-form Article (WriteFreely/Ghost), PeerTube video, Pixelfed image post, Lemmy threaded topic
  
*Structural:* quote posts require author consent and can be revoked — a quote edge that can be severed after capture · visibility is a per-status enum (public/unlisted/followers/direct), not an account setting · server heterogeneity: character limits, features, and retention differ per instance · one timeline carries structurally different ActivityPub object types (Note vs Article vs Video vs Question) · account migration moves follows but NOT posts — content is instance-bound

**Hacker News** — story (title + external URL); text story / Ask HN (title + text, no URL); Show HN (convention on story); job post (no comments); poll + pollopt (poll options are separate items); comment (nested tree); favorites (public saved list) and upvoted (private list)
  
*Structural:* official API item types are exactly: story, comment, job, poll, pollopt — the leanest schema in the domain · text-and-link only; zero native media · no quote/repost edges at all; only reply trees · short edit/delete window, then immutable; posts archived · karma-weighted ranking, flagged/dead states, second-chance re-ranking pool

**LinkedIn** — text post (≤3,000 chars); single-image / multi-image post (≤9 images); document post a.k.a. carousel (uploaded PDF/PPT/DOC rendered as swipeable deck); native video post (≤10–15 min); LinkedIn Live (real-time broadcast via third-party encoder); poll (2–4 options; 1 day–2 weeks); article (long-form ~110k chars, rich formatting, Google-indexed, permanent Articles tab); newsletter (serialized articles; subscribers get push + email per edition); event (RSVP object, often wrapping a Live); celebration/occasion template post; reshare with commentary; comment (one-level nesting) + typed reactions
  
*Structural:* document post is unique: an uploaded office file becomes an interactive carousel — the saved artifact is a FILE · articles and posts are two different content surfaces with different permalinks, limits, and indexing · newsletter = article + subscription/distribution layer (serialized parent container) · external links algorithmically suppressed → link-in-first-comment convention · events and lives are scheduled, time-bound objects

**Facebook** — text/status post (with background styles); photo post / album / carousel; native video post (≤240 min); Reel (short vertical video, ≤90s); Story (photo/video, 24h ephemeral, interactive stickers/polls); Live broadcast (replay optional); link post; poll (groups; story poll stickers); Note (2026 meaning: 60-char text bubble on Messenger/profile, 24h, no archive — old long-form blogging Notes retired 2020); group post (community-scoped, incl. anonymous posting in some groups); event (RSVP); marketplace listing; fundraiser; life-event/celebration template post; saved items with user-defined collections
  
*Structural:* same 'post' can live on profile, page, or group — container changes visibility and features · three separate ephemerality systems: Stories (24h, archived privately), Notes (24h, gone forever), Live (replay optional) · albums are a legacy persistent media container distinct from carousels · old rich-text Notes were deleted from servers — a warning case for archival products · share/reshare graph plus typed reactions

**Discord** — channel message (text + embeds + attachments + stickers); thread (branch off a message or channel, auto-archiving); forum channel post (title + tags + comment stream, gallery or list view); media channel post (gallery-style; often gated behind server subscriptions); announcement channel message (publishable/cross-postable to other servers); voice message; poll (≤10 answers, ≤32 days, multi-select; immutable after creation); stage channel session (live audio event); voice/video channel session (no artifact); pinned message; DM / group DM; server (guild) and category as nested containers
  
*Structural:* no public web permalinks — deep links resolve only for members; capture requires being inside · forum/media channels graft Reddit-style titled-post + comments onto chat infrastructure · messages arbitrarily editable/deletable; whole servers can vanish — highest volatility in the domain · paywalled media channels blur posts for non-subscribers · polls cannot be edited once created

**Pinterest** — pin (unified 2023 format: image, video, or mixed multi-asset; flexible ratios; title/description/link; music/stickers); collage (interactive composite of cutouts, each cutout independently tappable/shoppable); board (curated pin collection; collaborative/secret; board sections); rich pin (auto-syncs product/recipe/article metadata from source site); product-tagged pin / affiliate-link pin; legacy idea pin (multi-page slideshow — merged into unified Pin but corpus persists)
  
*Structural:* a pin is fundamentally a visual bookmark: image + outbound destination URL — the closest native analogue to Commonplace itself · rich pins mutate over time (price/availability auto-update from source) · save-to-board (repin) is the core social graph; comments are marginal · collage cutouts are sub-items with their own links — one pin, many targets · idea pins/video pins/standard pins collapsed into ONE format in 2023 — schema churn precedent

**Tumblr** — post as NPF block document (text/heading/image/GIF/video/audio/link/poll blocks — legacy 7 post types collapsed into blocks); reblog (re-publication carrying full ancestor trail + appended commentary); ask → answer post (two-party post, supports anonymous asker); poll (block within a post); community post (native Communities, 2024–25; comments + emoji reactions); tags (freeform, unlimited, primary discovery mechanism); queued / drafted / scheduled post states; reply and like
  
*Structural:* reblog trail: each reblog is a first-class post embedding the entire chain — quote-of-quote-of-quote as the norm · asks publish a question+answer pair as a single post authored by two parties (one possibly anonymous) · community posts cannot be reblogged out — a one-way content valve · Neue Post Format = ordered typed content blocks, not a single body field · Tumblr Live (Jan 2024) and Post+ shut down — ephemeral/paywall experiments retired

**Quora** — question (canonical, collaboratively edited, mergeable/redirectable); answer (per-author competing documents under one question, each with own comment thread); Space post (blog-style long-form inside a Space); share/link (URL shared into a Space with commentary); Space question (answerable only by Space contributors); Space (container with per-type content permissions); comment (threaded) + upvote/downvote; paywalled content (Quora+ conventional or ADAPTIVE paywall); AI-generated answer surfaced above human answers
  
*Structural:* question and answer are distinct node types: answers compete in ranked parallel, they don't discuss in a tree · questions merge/redirect — the canonical node a save points to can be rewritten or absorbed · adaptive paywall gates the SAME item algorithmically per-viewer — a saved item's accessibility is nondeterministic · all creator-payout programs retired (Partner Program 2023; Space Subscriptions & Ads Share Nov 2024); Quora+ is the only gate · AI answers injected at top change what 'the content at this URL' means over time

**Archetypes distilled:**
- **Micro-post in a reply/quote graph** — covers: X tweet/thread/reply/quote/repost, Threads post/ghost post/community post, Bluesky post, Mastodon status + boost + quote, Tumblr post + reblog trail, Facebook status/profile post, LinkedIn feed post + reshare. Key properties: short rich-text body + ordered media attachment set (images/GIF/video), author, timestamp; three edge types on one node: reply-to (tree), quote-of (embed + commentary — incl. Tumblr's recursive reblog trail as the extreme case), repost-of (identity); self-thread chains (ordered sibling sequence by same author); optional attachable blocks: poll, link card, location, topic tag; per-post permission gates (reply controls, quote controls, threadgates) and visibility scopes
- **Titled community submission + ranked comment tree** — covers: Reddit text/link/image/gallery/video/poll posts, Hacker News story/Ask HN/Show HN/job/poll, Discord forum & media channel posts, Facebook group posts, Quora Space posts & shares. Key properties: required-ish title + payload variant (link | text | media set | poll) — payload is a variant field, not a separate type (Reddit now allows body text on all types); community container namespace (subreddit/forum channel/group/Space) with per-container allowed types, flair/tag taxonomies, and rules; nested comment tree with score-based server-side sort orders and collapse states; vote/score as primary signal; awards/reactions secondary; crosspost/share edges between containers
- **Q&A: canonical question with competing answers** — covers: Quora question/answers, Quora Space questions, Reddit AMA & Ask HN (degenerate cases layered on archetype 2). Key properties: question is a canonical, collaboratively-edited node ownable by no one; it can be merged or redirected after you save it; answers are parallel sibling documents (not a tree), each with independent author, votes, and its own comment thread; ranking among answers is dynamic — 'the top answer' is a moving target; per-viewer paywall gating (Quora+ adaptive paywall) makes accessibility nondeterministic
- **Long-form article / newsletter edition** — covers: X Articles & 25k-char long-form posts, LinkedIn articles & newsletter editions, Quora Space long-form posts, fediverse Article objects (WriteFreely/Ghost via Mastodon timeline), Tumblr long text posts (NPF). Key properties: block-structured rich-text document (headings, inline media, embeds) with title/cover — needs a block model, not a string; stable dedicated permalink, often search-engine indexed, distinct from the author's feed surface; optional serialized parent (newsletter) with edition numbering and subscriber push/email distribution; paywall/subscriber gating variants (X Premium subscriber posts, Quora+); editable indefinitely — version drift after save
- **Curated collection container** — covers: Pinterest boards + sections, Bluesky starter packs / lists / custom feeds, Threads custom feeds, X lists & bookmark folders, Reddit multireddits/custom feeds, Facebook saved-item collections, Mastodon 'packs' (in development). Key properties: named container whose members are references (to posts, accounts, feeds, or communities), not copies; own permalink/identity — the collection itself is a shareable, saveable object; ordering, sections, collaborative editing, public/secret visibility; algorithmic variant: custom feeds are collections defined by rule rather than enumeration; membership churn — contents change after capture
- **Visual bookmark / pin (pointer + preview)** — covers: Pinterest pins, Pinterest collages (multi-target composite), rich pins (auto-syncing metadata), link posts generally (Reddit link post, HN story, FB link post) in their pointer aspect. Key properties: the item's essence is an outbound destination URL + visual preview + annotation — content lives elsewhere; metadata can auto-update from the source (rich pins: price, availability, recipe) — the saved object mutates; composites: one item, many sub-targets (collage cutouts each independently linked/shoppable); save/repin count is the dominant social signal; discussion is marginal; aligns exactly with Commonplace's own item model — schema should treat 'pointer + cached preview + annotations' as first-class
- **Chat message stream (gated, permalink-poor)** — covers: Discord channel messages / threads / voice messages / DMs, X DMs / X Chat, Threads DMs (incl. rerouted ghost-post replies), Bluesky chat, Facebook Messenger. Key properties: sequential untitled messages inside a membership-gated container (guild→channel→thread nesting); identity is (container, channel, message-id), not a public URL — capture requires membership context; branching threads, reactions, inline attachments/embeds/polls; arbitrary edit/delete; containers themselves can vanish — highest-volatility archetype, capture-time snapshot is the only durable record; no algorithmic feed; position in stream is the ordering
- **Live / ephemeral session** — covers: X Spaces & live video, LinkedIn Live & events, Facebook Live / Stories / 60-char Notes, Threads ghost posts (also archetype 1), Discord stage & voice sessions, Bluesky LIVE badge (external-stream pointer), Pinterest TV & Tumblr Live (retired — precedent). Key properties: validity window is intrinsic: scheduled start, live phase, then expiry or optional replay artifact; three post-expiry outcomes to model: replay artifact (recorded Space/Live), private archive (Stories, ghost posts), or total deletion (FB Notes, Discord voice); may have no permalink at all, or a permalink that dies — capture must happen inside the window; RSVP/schedule metadata (events) precedes content existence; frequently a pointer to an off-platform stream rather than hosted media

### Census domain 3: Web, publishing & non-platform (incl. local files)

**Arbitrary website / web page** — article-like page; landing/marketing page; JS-rendered SPA view (app shell, content only after hydration); infinite-scroll feed page (one URL, unbounded items); interactive tool/calculator/visualization; image/gallery page; forum or comment thread; data table / dataset page; page fragment (anchor or #:~:text= text-fragment deep link); page with embedded third-party media (YouTube/tweet iframes); login-gated web-app screen
  
*Structural:* dynamic/JS pages: DOM content differs from HTML source, capture needs a rendered snapshot · no schema guarantees: author, date, and even title are all optional · cookie/consent walls and geo-blocks intercept fetch · link rot and silent content drift: page changes or dies after save, so snapshot-at-capture is mandatory · canonical URL vs tracking-param variants (utm_*) — same content, many URLs · one URL can contain many saveable items (feeds) or the item can be a sub-page fragment

**Blog post (Substack / Medium / Ghost / WordPress / personal blog)** — long-form text post (free / truncated-preview paywalled / subscriber-only); Substack podcast episode post; Substack native video post; Substack live video (recording auto-saved as post; Substack TV app Jan 2026); Substack Notes / Ghost Notes (short-form social posts from a 'blog' platform); Substack chat thread; Medium article with audio narration + responses (comments-as-posts); Ghost 6 post = simultaneously web page + email newsletter + federated ActivityPub object (followable from Mastodon/Threads; Bluesky bridge); WordPress post/page/custom-post-type with comments
  
*Structural:* dual/triple publication: the same post exists as web page, email copy, and fediverse object — versions can differ · partial paywall: preview is public, body truncated · posts are edited after publish; RSS gives a parallel feed representation · author + timestamp are reliably present here (unlike arbitrary web pages)

**Bare URL / pasted or shared link** — unresolved URL string (content type unknown until fetched); shortened link (bit.ly, t.co — destination hidden); redirect-chain link (tracking wrapper); share-sheet payload (title + URL only); already-dead link; login-gated link (permalink exists but resolves to auth wall); action link, not a document (mailto:, tel:, app deep link)
  
*Structural:* the degenerate case: identity only, zero payload — schema must allow a reference-only item that is progressively enriched on resolution · unfurl metadata (OpenGraph/oEmbed) may be all that is ever obtainable · URL is not identity: shorteners, params, and redirects mean one work maps to many URLs and vice versa

**News articles** — standard article; live blog (reverse-chron entries continuously appended under one URL); explainer / Q&A format; photo essay; interactive / scrollytelling data piece; opinion / op-ed; video-led article; syndicated wire copy (identical content on many outlets' URLs)
  
*Structural:* paywalls in 4 modes — hard, metered, freemium/premium, dynamic (behavior-targeted); plus registration walls; industry shifting harder (free-only publishers down to ~6%) · articles mutate: updates, corrections, changed timestamps — a live blog is never 'done' · schema.org NewsArticle + isAccessibleForFree paywall markup often present · capture may only get the pre-wall stub; full text requires the user's session

**Newsletters / email** — editorial newsletter issue; link digest (one email = many saveable sub-items); transactional/receipt email; personal email worth saving; MIME multipart: text/plain + text/html + optional text/x-amp-html dynamic AMP part (rendered only by Gmail/Yahoo/Mail.ru, fetches fresh content at open time)
  
*Structural:* no public permalink by default — your copy lives in a private inbox; some issues have a 'view in browser' web twin · per-recipient personalization: your copy is not the canonical copy · every link is tracking-wrapped (redirect), images proxied/blocked · AMP part is mutable-at-open — the email you saved can render differently later · capture point is the inbox (IMAP/Gmail API/forward-in), not the web

**Wikipedia / wiki pages** — encyclopedia article; list article; disambiguation page; category page; talk/discussion page; revision-pinned permalink (oldid=) vs living current page; infobox (Wikidata-backed structured facts)
  
*Structural:* NO single author — collective, partly anonymous authorship breaks the creator field · perpetually mutating, but uniquely offers stable revision permalinks and full public history · dense internal link graph + cross-language variants of the 'same' page · CC BY-SA licensed — one of the few sources where storing full text is unambiguously permitted

**Documentation** — API reference page; guide/tutorial (ordered sequence — one page mid-sequence loses meaning alone); changelog/release notes; versioned doc page (same page × /v2/, /v3/ ...); auto-generated reference (OpenAPI, JSDoc); README / repo docs; man page
  
*Structural:* version-scoped truth: a saved doc page silently becomes wrong when the software moves on · frequent site restructures = URL breakage · usually no author and no date at all · code blocks are first-class content and need to survive extraction verbatim

**Academic papers (PDF / arXiv)** — arXiv preprint — now in PDF + auto-converted HTML (all TeX submissions since Dec 2023) + downloadable LaTeX source; publisher version of record (often paywalled); accepted-manuscript / OA repository copy; versioned identity (arXiv v1, v2, ...); abstract as standalone unit; figures/tables/equations (MathML in HTML); supplementary materials: datasets, code, video
  
*Structural:* identity is DOI/arXiv ID, not URL — one work = many URLs, files, formats, and versions with differing content · multi-author with affiliations; citation graph is core value · the paywalled VoR and the free preprint are the 'same' work but not identical text — schema needs work-level vs manifestation-level identity

**PDF document (generic)** — text-based PDF; scanned/image-only PDF (needs OCR); form PDF (AcroForm/XFA); slide deck exported to PDF; tagged/accessible PDF and PDF/A archival profile; password-protected/encrypted PDF
  
*Structural:* may arrive as a URL OR a local file — same object, two provenance modes · internal metadata (title/author fields) is frequently junk; no reliable creation date · fixed layout: annotation coordinate is page + region, not scroll position · multi-column/scan reading order breaks naive text extraction

**Ebook (EPUB)** — reflowable EPUB (EPUB 3.3, W3C — a ZIP of XHTML+CSS+images with OPF manifest and nav doc); fixed-layout EPUB (comics, photo books, magazines); EPUB with media overlays (text-synced narration); DRM-locked copy (Adobe/Kindle ecosystem) vs DRM-free
  
*Structural:* a book is a long-lived container, not one note: chapter spine structure, and reading position/highlights use CFI locators, not URLs · no inherent page numbers in reflowable layout · identity via ISBN/edition, not URL · DRM makes content legally/technically unparseable — schema must tolerate an item whose full text can never be extracted

**Google Docs / Notion pages** — Google Doc with document tabs + nested child tabs (up to 3 levels), comments/suggestions layer, revision history, linked Sheets charts; Notion block-tree page (paragraphs, headings, toggles, callouts, code, synced blocks, meeting-notes/transcription blocks, new interactive HTML blocks — Notion 3.6, Jul 2026); Notion database (a page that is a container of pages, with table/board/calendar/gallery/list/chart views); recursively nested child pages
  
*Structural:* auth-gated: a shareable link that anonymous fetch cannot resolve — a permalink that isn't public · live collaborative document: no publication moment, content moves constantly — capture = snapshot of a moving target at revision R · identity is block-level, not page-level; synced blocks put the same content in N places · a page can BE a database — item-vs-container is recursive

**Product / e-commerce pages (Amazon / Etsy / Shopify)** — product listing (title, price, spec table, brand A+ content); image gallery + product videos; 3D model / AR view (View in 3D, View in Your Room, Virtual Try-On); AI-generated audio: review summaries ('Hear the Highlights') and conversational audio Q&A on the detail page (launched Apr 2026); variants (size/color) — many sub-products under one URL; customer reviews + Q&A (UGC sub-content); schema.org/Product structured data
  
*Structural:* the fields the user actually cares about — price, availability — are volatile; needs field-level, timestamped snapshots · variant selection changes content without changing the URL · listing dies on delisting; marketplace duplicates put one product at N seller URLs · affiliate/tracking params pollute URL identity; page may be personalized per viewer

**Recipe websites** — recipe payload (schema.org/Recipe JSON-LD: name, image, ingredients, stepwise instructions, prep/cook time, yield, nutrition, ratings, nested video); surrounding narrative blog post (SEO life-story wrapper); print-view / jump-to-recipe variant of the same page
  
*Structural:* bimodal document: narrative wrapper vs machine-readable structured payload — save intent is almost always only the payload, and JSON-LD makes clean extraction feasible · Google requires/recommends the exact structured fields, so extraction is unusually reliable here · the structured part supports computation (scaling, unit conversion) that plain text does not

**Maps / place listings (Google Maps pin)** — place listing (name, address, coordinates, hours, phone, photos, reviews, popular times, menu); dropped pin — bare coordinates with NO listing, no name, no metadata; saved list / collaborative list of places (shareable, editable by invitees; Gemini auto-tagging of pins); user's own review/photos of a place; route/directions link
  
*Structural:* the referent is a PHYSICAL PLACE, not a document — no author, no timestamp, no 'content' in the document sense · the listing is an aggregate view that constantly changes (hours, reviews) and the place itself can close permanently · share links are shortened app deep links (maps.app.goo.gl); identity is place-ID or lat/lng, never a content URL · a dropped pin is the extreme case: coordinates only — the schema's minimal item

**LOCAL FILES — direct upload, no source platform (CRITICAL)** — camera-roll photo: HEIC/JPEG, ProRAW (DNG, 12/48MP), JPEG XL, portrait/depth photos; Live Photo = still + micro-video (single HEIC container or JPG + H.264 pair); screenshot (PNG; an image OF other content — a tweet, a chat, a paywalled page); voice memo (M4A/AAC; WhatsApp voice notes in Opus); downloaded/saved video file (MP4/MOV/WebM — often a re-encoded copy of platform content, e.g. watermarked TikTok save); scanned document (multi-page PDF or image of a PAPER artifact); user's own text files/notes (txt, md, docx)
  
*Structural:* NO platform, NO permalink, NO URL — identity must be a content hash · NO creator distinct from the user (or creator unknown); no title; timestamp is EXIF-or-mtime and lies after transfers (WhatsApp/Telegram strip EXIF) · meaning is locked inside the media: OCR for screenshots/scans, ASR for voice memos, vision for photos — a local item is unusable without an ML extraction pass · screenshots are second-order captures: the true source (platform, author, date) exists only latently inside the pixels — provenance must be inferable and marked as inferred · compound files: a Live Photo is two media in one logical item; a scan is N pages of one document

**Archetypes distilled:**
- **Canonical web document** — covers: blog posts (Substack/Medium/Ghost/WordPress), news articles (non-live), documentation pages, recipe pages, arbitrary article-like web pages, newsletter 'view in browser' twins. Key properties: permalink identity + extractable main content vs page chrome; author/timestamp usually present but MUST be nullable (docs pages have neither); mutates and rots after save — full snapshot at capture time is mandatory, with capture_date distinct from published_date; access modifiers: paywall (hard/metered/dynamic/regwall), consent wall, JS-render requirement — capture fidelity is a per-item fact worth recording; may carry structured-data payloads (JSON-LD Recipe/Product/NewsArticle) that should be extracted alongside prose
- **Unresolved reference** — covers: bare pasted URL, shortened/tracking-wrapped links, dropped map pin (coordinates only), DOI/ISBN strings, dead links, share-sheet title+URL payloads. Key properties: identity only, zero or near-zero payload — the schema's minimal legal item; progressive enrichment lifecycle: unresolved → unfurled (OG metadata) → fully captured → dead; resolution can fail, redirect, or change over time; store both the given URL and the resolved canonical one; forces the rule: NO field except identity + save-timestamp may be required
- **Versioned work (multi-manifestation)** — covers: academic papers (arXiv v1/v2 × PDF/HTML/LaTeX × preprint/VoR), versioned documentation (/v2/, /v3/), ebook editions, syndicated wire articles (one work, many outlet URLs). Key properties: two-level identity: abstract WORK (DOI/arXiv ID/ISBN) vs concrete MANIFESTATION (a specific file/URL/version) — flattening these corrupts dedup and citation; versions have genuinely different content; 'latest' is a moving pointer; citation/reference graph between works is first-class value; the free copy and the canonical copy may differ (preprint vs paywalled VoR)
- **Bounded rich file (document container)** — covers: PDF (text, scanned, form, deck), EPUB (reflowable, fixed-layout, media-overlay, DRM), DOCX and other office files, scanned documents. Key properties: self-contained binary with internal structure: pages/chapters/spine — annotation coordinates are internal locators (page+region, EPUB CFI), never scroll offsets or URLs; dual provenance: same file can arrive via URL or local upload — file identity (hash) must be separable from acquisition source; may require OCR / may be DRM- or password-locked — schema must tolerate items whose text is permanently unextractable; internal metadata fields are unreliable; long-lived engagement (a book is read over weeks, with many positions/highlights per item)
- **Living document (collaborative / revisioned)** — covers: Google Docs (tabs, comments, revision history), Notion pages and databases (block trees, synced blocks), wikis/Wikipedia (public, collective-author variant), live blogs (append-only news variant). Key properties: no publication moment — content is under continuous mutation; a save is a snapshot pinned to a revision/time; identity below page level: blocks/tabs/sections; synced blocks put one block in N pages; recursive containment: a page can be a database of pages — item vs collection is not a fixed distinction; auth-gated permalinks (Docs/Notion): the link exists but public fetch fails — capture requires the user's session; Wikipedia is the public variant with no single author but stable revision permalinks
- **Entity listing (aggregate view of a real-world referent)** — covers: product pages (Amazon/Etsy/Shopify), place listings and map pins with metadata (Google Maps), by extension: job posts, real-estate listings, event pages. Key properties: the referent is a THING or PLACE, not a document — 'creator' and 'published date' are category errors here; key fields are volatile and are exactly what the user cares about (price, availability, hours, open/closed) — needs typed field-level snapshots with timestamps, not just a text blob; variant/sub-entity structure under one URL; UGC sub-content (reviews, Q&A) attached to the entity; hard death: delisting/closure removes the referent, not just the page; identity = platform entity-ID (ASIN, place-ID) or coordinates, not URL
- **Private inbox item** — covers: newsletters (Substack/Ghost email copies, standalone newsletters), personal/transactional email worth saving, by extension: DMs and chat messages forwarded in. Key properties: no public permalink — the copy exists only in the user's inbox; identity = Message-ID; capture point is the mailbox, not the web; per-recipient personalization and tracking-wrapped links: the saved copy is not the canonical copy and its URLs need unwrapping; MIME multipart with divergent renderings (plain/HTML/AMP); the AMP part fetches fresh content at open — even email is mutable; digest issues are containers: one email yields many saveable sub-items
- **Local capture (platform-less binary)** — covers: camera-roll photos (HEIC/ProRAW/Live Photo/JPEG XL), screenshots, voice memos, downloaded videos, scans, user's own notes/files. Key properties: breaks every platform assumption at once: no platform, no permalink, no URL, no creator, no title — identity MUST be content-hash; creator is null or the user themself; timestamps are EXIF/mtime with a confidence problem (messaging apps strip EXIF; transfers reset mtime) — schema needs timestamp + provenance-confidence, not a bare datetime; content meaning exists only inside the media: OCR/ASR/vision extraction is a required pipeline stage, and extracted text/provenance must be stored as INFERRED, separate from ground-truth metadata; second-order provenance: a screenshot or watermarked video download is a capture OF platform content — the schema needs an inferred_source slot so a local file can later link to the platform item it depicts; compound media: Live Photo (still+video), multi-page scans — one logical item, multiple physical assets

### Census domain 4: Rich / structured / edge-case media

**Twitch** — live stream; past broadcast / VOD; highlight; clip (5-60s); upload; chat replay (sidecar)

*Structural:* Bluesky poll lexicon still an open atproto PR — absence of a type is itself a schema fact (verified) · federation: the same Mastodon post has different URLs on every instance — canonical-ID vs viewed-URL split · Bluesky items have dual identity: at:// URI vs https URL

**Google Maps / Apple Maps / outdoors apps (AllTrails, Strava, Komoot)** — place pin (place_id + coords + live attributes); saved list (Starred/Favorites/Want to go + custom, with per-place notes and map icons); collaborative shared list; directions/route link (parameterized query); My Maps custom map (KML layers); review/photo attached to a place; Street View / immersive view; Apple Maps Guide; trail page / saved route / GPX activity
  
*Structural:* lists carry per-item annotations and render onto the live map (Google Maps Help, howtogeek 2025) · collaborative lists mutate under you (androidauthority 2025) · a place is a living entity: hours, closure, even coordinates drift; place_id vs lat/lng dual identity · a route is a query, not a document — re-running it gives different results

**Shopping (Amazon, Etsy, eBay, TikTok Shop, Instagram Shopping, Google Shopping)** — product listing (title/price/variants/images/video); wishlist / registry / watchlist (shareable container); review (authored sub-item on a listing); storefront / shop page; video PDP / LIVE shopping session; cart-saved-for-later item
  
*Structural:* price, stock, and variants are volatile — a save needs snapshot-at-time plus stable product ID (ASIN/GTIN) · variant matrix problem: which size/color did you actually save? · delisting cascades: Instagram removes delisted products from YOUR saved list (Meta help, verified) · wishlists are shareable containers with view-only links

**Events (Eventbrite, Meetup, Luma, Partiful, Facebook Events, Ticketmaster, calendar .ics)** — event page (time/venue/host/RSVP); recurring event series; calendar subscription (iCal feed); .ics file; ticket/order confirmation; RSVP state (per-user)
  
*Structural:* hard lifecycle: upcoming → happening → past; the page's meaning inverts after the date · details mutate (venue/time changes) after you save · ICS is the universal interchange — Luma and Partiful both export/sync it (help.luma.com, help.partiful.com, verified) · RSVP is viewer-specific state layered on shared content · recurrence rules make one 'event' many instances

**Messaging & DMs (WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord, iMessage, Messenger, Signal)** — starred message (WhatsApp); Saved Messages self-chat with tags (Telegram); Later saved item with In-progress/Archived/Completed states (Slack); bookmarked message (Discord, Nitro-only, 200-message cap); pinned message; shared link/photo/file/voice note inside a chat; view-once / disappearing media; channel post (WhatsApp Channels, Telegram channels); chat thread itself
  
*Structural:* no public permalink; access requires an authenticated session — a pointer-based save is worthless, content must be copied out at save time · meaning depends on surrounding conversation context and sender identity · view-once/disappearing policies delete the referent (Telegram one-time voice/video, verified) · each platform reinvented private-save differently: star vs self-forward vs Later-with-workflow-states vs capped bookmarks (all verified against vendor docs)

**Device camera roll & screen captures (iOS/Android/desktop)** — screenshot of a tweet/post/chat/article; screen recording; photo of a menu / poster / whiteboard / receipt; photo of a book page; scanned document; live photo
  
*Structural:* the file is an image but the CONTENT is a reference to something else — resolution requires OCR + entity linking · referent may be a resolvable URL (the tweet), a physical object (the menu), or already gone · zero provenance metadata: no source URL, no author, only EXIF time/place · this is the single largest 'save anywhere' channel and every item in it is a capture-of-a-capture

**Text-dense images: infographics, memes, comics strips (Pinterest, r/coolguides, GIPHY, Tenor, Imgur, KnowYourMeme, xkcd, GoComics)** — infographic (document-as-pixels); meme image (template + instance text); GIF (video-as-image; often mp4/webp behind a .gif URL); sticker; multi-panel comic strip; Imgur album (container)
  
*Structural:* semantic payload is inside pixels — searchability depends entirely on OCR/vision extraction, not metadata · memes have template/instance duality (KnowYourMeme catalogs templates) and are reposted everywhere — dedup must happen at content level, not URL · GIF format identity is a lie: the same 'GIF' is served as mp4, webm, or webp

**Webtoons & serialized comics (WEBTOON, Tapas, Manta, Marvel Unlimited)** — series (container); episode (vertical-scroll canvas of stitched images); Daily Pass / Fast Pass / Ad Pass unlocked episode; creator post

**Quizzes, surveys & interactive posts (BuzzFeed, Kahoot, Google Forms, Typeform, Strawpoll, story stickers, YouTube quizzes)** — personality quiz (input → personalized result); knowledge quiz with answer key; survey/form (input collector); standalone poll; prediction market question

**Living documents & design files (Google Docs/Sheets/Slides, Notion, Figma/FigJam, Canva, Miro, Airtable, Loom)** — document / spreadsheet / deck (shared by link); Notion page/database; Figma file / prototype / FigJam board; Canva design; Miro board; Airtable base/view; Loom video with transcript + comments
  
*Structural:* content at save-time ≠ content at read-time — the document keeps being edited; platforms have revision history but the saver doesn't control it · access-gated links (workspace permissions) — pointer may die silently · deep internal structure (blocks, frames, sheets) resists flattening to text

**Interactive web documents (scrollytelling articles, Tableau Public, ArcGIS StoryMaps, Genially, itch.io games, data dashboards)** — scrollytelling / interactive article; embedded dashboard; story map; browser game; explorable explanation
  
*Structural:* behavior and data queries are the content; a static snapshot captures one state of many · JS-dependent — dead without its runtime

**Facebook (residual distinct types)** — photo album (container); event; Marketplace listing; group post; story; reel

**Music/podcast containers (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music) — container aspect only** — playlist (mutable, ordered, sometimes collaborative); album; podcast show → episode hierarchy; Blend / algorithmic playlist
  
*Structural:* children are DRM-locked — you can only ever save references, never payloads · algorithmic playlists regenerate: same URL, different contents every week — the container has no stable state

**Archetypes distilled:**
- **CONTAINER-OF-ITEMS — a parent object whose children are themselves items** — covers: Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn carousels, X/Reddit threads (ordered chains, trees), YouTube/Spotify/Sketchfab playlists, Pinterest boards + sections, photo albums (FB, Imgur), gists & 3D-print 'things' (multi-file bundles), GitHub star Lists, Google Maps saved lists. Key properties: parent identity + ordered or keyed child membership; children are full items of OTHER archetypes (schema must recurse); membership and order mutate after save — need list-state-at-time vs live-list distinction; save-scope ambiguity: user may mean the container, one child (slide 7), or a span — schema needs child-level addressing; per-child annotations exist natively (gallery captions, map-list notes) and must not be flattened away; containers nest (board → section → pin) and can be collaborative (membership changes by other people)
- **REFERENCE-CAPTURE — a saved thing that is itself a capture/pointer to another thing** — covers: screenshot of a tweet/chat/article, photo of a menu / book page / poster, Pinterest pin (surrogate image + source URL), quote posts, retweets, crossposts, Twitch/YouTube clips (offset+duration into a parent), links shared into chats, TikTok's own photo-post save (rendered video of original images), memes as instances of templates. Key properties: two-layer identity: the ARTIFACT you hold vs the REFERENT it depicts — schema needs both slots plus a resolution-status field; referent may be: resolvable to a canonical item (the tweet), physical/offline (the menu), or already dead — three different lifecycle handlings; provenance chains exceed two layers (screenshot of a quote-tweet of a TikTok) — model as a chain, not a pair; dedup and cross-analysis must key on the resolved referent, not the artifact URL/hash; extraction (OCR, entity linking, timestamp parsing) is the enrichment path from artifact to referent
- **EPHEMERAL / TIME-BOXED OBJECT — content with an expiry or state machine** — covers: stories (IG/Snapchat/WhatsApp status) — 24h, live streams (Twitch/YouTube/TikTok LIVE/IG Live) — scheduled→live→VOD, X Spaces — replay only 30 days, host-dependent, Twitch VODs — 7/14/60-day tiered expiry, view-once / disappearing messages, Webtoon Daily/Ad Pass unlocks (14-day / 3-day access), Marketplace listings (sold→gone). Key properties: lifecycle state machine (scheduled → live → replay → expired) — the same identity changes form and URL across states; schema needs state + state-timestamps; capture-window constraint: payload must be snapshotted before TTL; post-hoc capture is impossible (verified: X Spaces, Twitch VODs, IG stories); expiry can apply to ACCESS not content (Webtoon pass unlocks) — save may hold a receipt to something you can no longer open; replay artifact ≠ live artifact (chat/audience context lost) — record which state was captured
- **STATEFUL INTERACTIVE OBJECT — content whose state accrues from audience interaction** — covers: polls (X, LinkedIn, Mastodon, Reddit, IG story stickers, YouTube community), quizzes (knowledge + personality), surveys/forms, Reddit predictions, petitions. Key properties: content = prompt + options + AGGREGATE state that changes until a close time (X: 5min–7d; LinkedIn: 1d–2w — verified); viewer-specific state (your vote, your quiz result) is distinct from shared state and from the author-visible state (LinkedIn authors see individual voters); snapshot-at-save ≠ final results — schema needs re-poll-at-close or at least a 'results final?' flag; results may be gated behind interaction (must vote to see) — capture may be structurally blind to part of the content; forms are pure input-endpoints: nothing meaningful to capture but the questions
- **LIVING VERSIONED ARTIFACT — content that keeps changing after you save it, with addressable versions** — covers: GitHub repos / files / gists / PRs, Google Docs/Sheets/Slides, Notion pages, Figma/Canva/Miro files, wikis, code sandboxes & notebooks, collaborative lists (Maps, playlists). Key properties: save must pin a version ref (commit SHA, revision ID) AND snapshot content — a bare URL is a pointer to a moving target; deep internal structure (file trees, blocks, frames, cells) — flat-text capture loses the item's shape; schema needs a structured-payload slot; often access-gated (workspace permissions) — pointer validity is per-viewer and can die silently; sub-file addressing is native (line ranges, block IDs, frame IDs) and users cite at that granularity
- **EXTERNAL-ENTITY RECORD — a page describing a real-world referent whose attributes drift** — covers: product listings (Amazon/Etsy/eBay/TikTok Shop), place pins (Google/Apple Maps), event pages (Eventbrite/Luma/Partiful/FB), trail/route pages, job postings, real-estate listings, books/recipes as records. Key properties: dual identity: stable entity ID (ASIN/GTIN, place_id, event ID, ISBN) + volatile attributes (price, stock, hours, date, open/closed) — store both, timestamp the attributes; variant dimension (size/color, session/date) — 'which one did you save' must be capturable; delisting cascades into user saves (verified: Instagram deletes delisted products from your saved list) — your library must outlive the platform's record; lifecycle semantics differ from content: an event page's meaning inverts after the date; recurrence rules multiply identity; interchange formats exist and should be captured (ICS for events, schema.org/Product, GPX for routes)
- **CONVERSATION-CONTEXT ITEM — content locked inside a private thread** — covers: DM messages and media (WhatsApp/Telegram/iMessage/Messenger/Signal), Slack Later items, Discord bookmarks, starred/pinned messages, links & files shared into chats, email (structurally identical). Key properties: no public permalink; access is session-gated — capture MUST copy payload out at save time, a pointer save is dead on arrival; provenance = sender + thread + timestamp; meaning depends on surrounding messages — schema needs a context-window slot; platform save mechanisms are wildly inconsistent (star / self-forward / Later-with-states / capped Nitro bookmarks — all verified) so the extension is the unifier; disappearing-message policies can delete the referent after you save the pointer; a link shared in chat is simultaneously this archetype AND a reference-capture — archetypes compose
- **RUNTIME-DEPENDENT MEDIA — content with no faithful static form** — covers: 3D models (glTF/USDZ/STL) + Sketchfab viewer, AR effects/lenses (executable), interactive dashboards & scrollytelling, browser games, prototypes (Figma), notebooks with live outputs. Key properties: needs a viewer/runtime to be experienced — capture strategy is a pair: (a) asset bundle + format IDs for future rendering, (b) rendered surrogate (screenshot/turntable video/oEmbed embed) for immediate use; multi-format identity: one model ships as USDZ for iOS AND glTF for Android (Sketchfab, verified) — format list, not single mime-type; multi-file bundles (geometry + textures + instructions) — payload is a directory, not a file; oEmbed/embed URLs are the practical cross-platform capture handle (Sketchfab implements oEmbed, verified)
- **TEXT-BEARING RASTER — a document masquerading as an image** — covers: infographics, memes, multi-panel comics & webtoon episodes, LinkedIn document-post pages, screenshots (overlaps reference-capture), photos of menus/book pages (overlaps reference-capture), scanned documents. Key properties: all semantic content is in pixels — search/analysis depends entirely on OCR + layout/vision extraction; schema needs an extracted-text+structure sidecar as a first-class field, not an afterthought; tall/multi-panel layouts (webtoon canvases, infographics) break thumbnail-and-caption assumptions; template/instance duality for memes — same template, thousands of instances; content-level (not URL-level) dedup; format identity is unreliable (a '.gif' may be mp4/webp) — sniff and record actual codec

## Part 2 — adversarial fit-test


### Batch A: video/audio + text/social archetypes

**Verdict:** The schema's spine — Save/Post/Referent + Signals + evidence-anchored Extractions — survives the graph-shaped social archetypes (quote/reply/repost edges, self-threads, Reddit trees) and the entity-groundable media happy path (a Spotify track → MusicBrainz NamedEntity) with modest extensions. But under adversarial instantiation it breaks on six fronts, four of them in REQUIRED fields or identity semantics that cannot be backfilled after mass ingestion: (1) mediaKind inherits oEmbed's four types (photo|video|link|rich) and oEmbed never defined "audio" — the enum literally cannot describe a music track, podcast episode, or TikTok sound without lying; (2) permalink-REQUIRED-unique fails for ephemeral stories (URL is login-gated and dead in 24h), view-once snaps (no URL ever exists), and live-phase streams (only URL is the channel URL, which collides across saves); (3) there is no lifecycle state machine, so everything time-boxed (Twitch 7/14/60-day VOD decay, IG 24h TTL, YouTube Premieres' scheduled→live→VOD inversion, poll close times) is unrepresentable; (4) there is no interactive-payload structure — a poll's options, vote counts, close time, and finality have no field anywhere, and shoehorning them into a StructuredContent Referent violates the schema's own aboutness/manifestation layer separation; (5) the claimed "cross-platform join key" (canonicalId+permalink) is per-manifestation, and the Referent union models aboutness only — so the same podcast episode saved on Spotify and Apple (whose true join key is the RSS episode GUID, which the spec REQUIRES to be identical across feeds) can never dedup: the WEMI Work layer is named but never instantiated; (6) Post has no ordered-heterogeneous-children mechanism, so a 3-segment story (or any carousel) is either 3 orphan Posts or 1 Post that can't hold its media. The saving grace: every fix is additive (enum values, nullable fields, one new node type, an open edge vocabulary) — nothing requires restructuring the WEMI split — but the blocking six must land before ingestion because they sit in identity and required-field semantics.

- **short video (TikTok/Reels)** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: TikTok sound is a saveable first-class audio entity: unrepresentable as Post (mediaKind lacks 'audio') and ungroundable as NamedEntity (original sounds have no MusicBrainz/Wikidata ID; platform-native soundId is not an accepted grounding authority); video→sound relationship ('uses this audio') is not expressible: references[] vocabulary is only quote/reply/repost; same for remix/stitch/duet edges; Sora-class AI provenance (C2PA manifest: model, generation timestamp, signature — verified embedded in Sora 2 output) is a CROSS-platform standard, but the only home is per-platform platformExtras{}, which defeats cross-platform analysis; the content_provenance facet is an inference layer, not a slot for structured credentials
- **long video (YouTube)** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: chapters / key moments (creator-authored AND platform-auto-generated — verified YouTube feature) have no home: extractions require role+grounding, signals carry no ordered titled segments; Premieres invert the lifecycle (pre-recorded asset passes through a scheduled live phase — verified): no state field, single createdAt is ambiguous between upload, premiere start, and publication; no title field in the base object as specified (only body/text) — title vs description are distinct semantics on every video platform
- **live stream (Twitch)** — fit: `gap`; stress: REQUIRED-unique permalink is unfillable during the live phase (channel URL collides across saves) — blocking; no lifecycle state machine: live/archived/expired states, scheduledStart, actualStart, archiveExpiresAt (Twitch VOD decay 7/14/60d verified), capturedDuringState — all missing; same event has two identities (live channel URL, then VOD id) with no version_of/same_event link
- **podcast / audio episode** — fit: `gap`; stress: mediaKind cannot be truthfully filled — 'audio' does not exist in the oEmbed-derived enum (verified against oembed.com spec §2.3.4); cross-platform identity failure: canonicalId+permalink is per-manifestation; the real work-level key (RSS episode GUID, required identical across feeds) has no field — WEMI Work is named in the design but never instantiated as a node; Referent kinds cannot stand in for 'the work itself': they model what content is ABOUT; grounding an arbitrary episode as NamedEntity yields externalId:NIL (no external authority covers most episodes)
- **music track** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: mediaKind: no 'audio' — the archetype's defining property is inexpressible in a required field; every music save must lie ('rich') or fail validation; createdAt semantics wobble: release date vs recording date vs platform-added date for re-released catalog music; cover/remix/sampled-in relations don't fit quote/reply/repost edges
- **threaded discussion (Reddit)** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: no title field — the archetype's required primary field; no community/container scope field (subreddit ≠ profile ≠ actor); vote/score is cross-platform and time-varying but has only a per-platform, untimestamped home — kills cross-analysis and misdates the signal
- **microblog self-thread (X / Bluesky)** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: no thread-grouping / save-scope object: 'the thread' as a saved unit is a derivation, not a stored fact; edit history: one logical post → multiple canonicalIds (edit_history_tweet_ids, verified) — no versions[] model, ambiguous which ID is 'canonical'; reply controls / threadgates / visibility scopes (X reply limits, Bluesky threadgates — same concept, three platforms) have no base field; burying them in platformExtras hides a cross-platform semantic
- **quote / repost graph** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: reference edges carry no target-snapshot or resolution status — deleted/blocked/withheld quote targets become dangling pointers with no tombstone record; no capture contract for trails: one page view implies N materialized Posts (Tumblr trail), with trail-order semantics no field preserves; quote-permission gates ('who can quote this') — cross-platform semantic with only a platformExtras home
- **poll (stateful interactive object)** — fit: `gap`; stress: no interactive-payload structure at all: options, counts, totalVotes, closesAt, resultsFinal, viewerVote — zero fields (blocking); mediaKind has no legal value for a poll; snapshot-at-save ≠ final results (5min–7d windows, verified) — no results-final flag or re-poll-at-close hook
- **ephemeral story (IG/Snap)** — fit: `gap`; stress: REQUIRED-unique permalink unfillable (dead-in-24h URLs; view-once snaps have none) — blocking; no TTL/lifecycle: expiresAt, active/expired/highlighted state, story→Highlight promotion with identity continuity (verified behavior) — blocking; no ordered-heterogeneous-children: N segments per story, each with own media+timestamp — renditions[] and collectionMembership are both the wrong tool

### Batch B: web/publishing/non-platform + local files

**Verdict:** The schema's ANALYSIS layer is genuinely strong for Batch B — SIGNALS/EXTRACTIONS with required evidence handles OCR'd scans, ASR'd voice memos, recipe slot-filling, and news claims (REPORTED assertion_mode) with no changes — but the CONTAINER layer breaks exactly where the founder feared, because its base invariants were written for platform social posts and are enforced as hard gates: (1) canonicalId+permalink "MANDATORY + unique in every profile" plus the base SHACL shape requiring creator, datePublished, and body-or-media (with failure meaning "the item cannot enter the pooled corpus" — the repo doc's own words, lines 288/343/346 of 2026-07-07-schema-foundations-research.md) are violated wholesale by every local file and partially by docs pages, products, places, and unresolved links; (2) mediaKind={photo|video|rich|link} is verified against the live oEmbed spec to have NO audio/document/file value, so a voice memo, PDF, or EPUB cannot be honestly represented at all; (3) the WEMI Work slot was spent on aboutness (Referent), leaving no identity for "which work is this a manifestation of" — so arXiv v5 vs v7, preprint vs VoR, syndicated news, and email+web newsletter editions cannot dedup or join; (4) evidence selectors cover only text-quote/position and AV #t=/#xywh= fragments, which W3C Media Fragments explicitly scopes to audio/video/images — there is no page, CFI, or structural selector, so nothing can cite inside a PDF, EPUB, or Notion block. All of it is fixable now, pre-migration, with six additive changes: typed-URI identity (platform-ID | resolved URL | RFC 6920 ni: hash | urn:uuid) with uniqueness scoped by versionRef; three new mediaKinds (audio, document, file) keeping oEmbed-4 as an export view; honest-null wrappers ({value|ASSERTED_NONE|UNKNOWN} + timestamp source/confidence) with the SHACL core shrunk to identity+savedAt; workRef/versionRef on Post; a per-Post capture record (captureStatus, accessGate, capturedAt); and adopting the full W3C Web Annotation selector suite (FragmentSelector+conformsTo+refinedBy) that the doc already cites but only imported 3 of 9 selectors from.

- **Canonical web document (arbitrary web page)** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: creator/datePublished must be nullable-with-semantics; SHACL base shape currently ejects author-less pages from the corpus; no captureStatus/accessGate field — paywalled/dynamic pages' missing body is indistinguishable from truly empty content; no givenUrl vs resolvedUrl (redirects, rel=canonical, AMP variants) — single permalink field conflates them
- **Long-form article / newsletter edition** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: body is a string — census demands a block model; workable path: HTML snapshot as rendition + extracted text as VERBAL_TEXT signal, but inline-image evidence loses its position in the document; email+web dual manifestation needs a work-level join (gap G4); edition numbering/serialized parent fits collectionMembership only informally
- **Bare link / unresolved reference** — fit: `gap`; stress: canonicalId required-at-write forces a provisional value into the permanent unique join key; resolution can change canonicalId later — mutating a REQUIRED UNIQUE key after thousands of extractions reference it is exactly the migration the founder fears; no resolutionStatus enum; no dead-link terminal state
- **News article (updated / syndicated)** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: datePublished vs dateModified vs capturedAt — one createdAt field cannot hold them; syndication dedup requires work identity (gap G4); JSON-LD NewsArticle is not in the StructuredContent shell list {Recipe/HowTo/ItemList/ExercisePlan}
- **PDF / academic paper (versioned work)** — fit: `gap`; stress: mediaKind unrepresentable (blocking, gap G2); work vs version identity absent (blocking, gap G4); no page/region selector for evidence (gap G6)
- **Ebook (bounded rich file)** — fit: `gap`; stress: mediaKind gap (G2) + hash identity gap (G1) + CFI selector gap (G6) all hit at once; weeks-long engagement: many highlights/positions per item — Save{tags,collections} has no annotation slot (gap G11); ISBN→OpenLibrary works as a Referent, but that models 'about Frankenstein' — the fact that this FILE IS a manifestation of that work has no first-class slot
- **Product / e-commerce listing** — fit: `gap`; stress: no product identity authority in the grounding spine (GTIN/ASIN); no typed field-level snapshots with timestamps (price/availability) — census demands them; StructuredContent shell list lacks schema.org/Product; variant dimension ('which color did you save') has no slot outside platformExtras
- **Map / place listing** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: null creator/datePublished must be legal and honest (gap G3); volatile attributes (hours, open/closed) need timestamped observations (gap G7); hard death: closure removes the referent — lifecycle state gap (G9)
- **Recipe site (structured content)** — fit: `clean`; stress: NYT Cooking variant is paywalled — inherits the shared captureStatus gap (G5), not a recipe-specific break; recipe pages get edited (ingredient swaps) — inherits version-drift gap; creator sometimes absent on aggregator recipe pages — inherits G3
- **Wiki / doc page (living document)** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: version pin vs work join is a forced loss without workRef+versionRef (gap G4); Wikipedia has no single author — creator must support null/collective (gap G3); auth-gated permalinks: pointer valid only per-viewer — accessGate + captureStatus (gap G5)
- **Local screenshot (reference-capture)** — fit: `gap`; stress: identity: needs urn:uuid item ID + RFC 6920 ni:sha-256 content hash as canonicalId (gap G1); no 'captures/depicts' edge with resolutionStatus for artifact→referent linking, chainable for screenshot-of-quote-tweet-of-TikTok (gap G8); timestamp needs {value, source=exif|file_mtime|user|inferred, confidence} — base fields have no provenance, only extractions do (gap G3)
- **Local photo (EXIF / compound Live Photo)** — fit: `gap`; stress: compound item identity (gap G1): item UUID + per-asset ni: hashes; media:hash verified md5/sha-1 only — insufficient alone (gap G10); timestamp provenance wrapper (gap G3); creator='self' vs 'unknown' vs 'none' — three distinct truths, one nullable field today
- **Local voice memo (audio file)** — fit: `gap`; stress: mediaKind=audio does not exist (gap G2) — the single clearest enum break in the batch; identity/platform/creator breaks shared with all local files (G1, G3); the degradation ladder 'every post resolves to at least link' (doc line 343) has no rung for a file with no link — ladder must bottom at file+hash
- **Scanned document (multi-page local capture)** — fit: `gap`; stress: page-aware selectors (G6) — without them every extraction from a multi-page scan has un-citable evidence, violating the schema's own evidence[1..N] REQUIRED rule in spirit; multi-page = compound asset like Live Photo (G1); physical referent: resolution-status vocabulary must include 'offline/physical', not just resolved/dead

### Batch C: rich/structured/recursive (containers, capture-of-capture)

**Verdict:** The schema survives Batch C's flat media (infographic is its best showing — VISUAL_TEXT signals + media_spatial selectors + INFERRED assertion mode were built exactly for text-in-pixels, and renditions[] is a perfect fit for GIF's format-identity lie) but BREAKS on the batch's two defining structures: PLURALITY and REFLEXIVITY. (1) One Post cannot hold N ordered heterogeneous children — the only candidate slot, renditions[] (Media RSS media:group), is spec-defined as "the same content, yet different representations," so a 20-slide Instagram carousel or 35-image TikTok photo post is structurally unrepresentable without corrupting rendition semantics; playlists/boards/series additionally lack a membership edge with order, snapshot-vs-live state, per-member annotation, and nesting. (2) The recursive capture-of-a-capture collapses twice: there is no depicts/capturesContent edge (references[] is reserved for platform-asserted quote/reply/repost, and the Referent union has no arm for "another piece of content"), and Post's REQUIRED-unique canonicalId+permalink makes it illegal to create the phantom Post that a resolved screenshot-of-a-tweet needs to hang its entities/claims on — violating the census's own rule that nothing beyond identity+save-timestamp may be required. A third cross-cutting weakness is TEMPORALITY: no version pin (code/living docs), no lifecycle/access state (delisted products, Webtoon Ad Pass 3-day expiry), no timestamped attribute observations (price/availability), no base-level interactive payload (polls exist on 7+ platforms but live only in platformExtras, killing the product's core cross-analysis promise). The good news: all fixes are additive (new edge types, new optional blocks, one relaxed constraint) — no existing field changes — so fixing now is cheap and fixing after ingestion is not.

- **Code repo/file (living versioned artifact)** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: No version/revision pin on Post — branch URL vs SHA-pinned permalink are different saves and the schema can't tell them apart; mediaKind {photo|video|rich|link} says nothing about a source file ('rich' garbage bucket); Signal channel for code is ambiguous: VERBAL_TEXT is natural-language-shaped; code is neither speech nor metadata
- **Multi-item CONTAINER (carousel / playlist / board / album / thread-as-unit)** — fit: `gap`; stress: renditions[] semantics spec-violated if used for slides — corrupts both the carousel AND legitimate rendition use; Per-slide extraction evidence has no anchor: media_spatial #xywh= against WHICH slide? Selectors lack a child/asset target reference; Save vs collection confusion is real: Save.collections (user's folders) vs Post.collectionMembership (platform playlists) are different universes; capturing a platform playlist into Save.collections pollutes the user's own organization
- **Interactive poll/quiz (stateful interactive object)** — fit: `gap`; stress: Results are time-varying: snapshot-at-save ≠ final; no resultsAsOf, no resultsFinal flag, no closesAt on base; Viewer-specific state (my vote, my quiz score) vs aggregate state vs author-visible state are three scopes; schema has zero; Gated visibility (must vote to see results) means capture can be structurally blind — no captureFidelity/completeness field to record that
- **Event (page for a scheduled real-world occurrence)** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: Meaning inversion after the date: queryable only if startDate is a typed slot — depends on the Event shell existing; Recurrence (weekly run club): RRULE multiplies identity; 'which occurrence did I save' hits the missing Save-level selector; Attribute drift (price/venue changes after save) needs observedAt timestamps on slot values — none exist
- **Geo route / place list** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: Non-AV asset slot missing (GPX here; USDZ/glTF, PDF, ICS elsewhere) — renditions[] is the wrong shape; No geo selector type; the selector registry is closed where source_role is extensible — inconsistent; Authority-policy tension: Google Places is proprietary yet sits in the grounding spine while the policy says CC0/CC-BY; OSM (the natural open geo authority) is ODbL and thus excluded — needs an explicit identifiers-only carve-out
- **Product wishlist** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: Volatile attributes need {value, observedAt} pairs, not bare slots — the user's actual interest IS the volatile field; Variant dimension ('which color did I save') needs a variant slot plus the missing Save-level selector; Delisting cascade requires Post.lifecycle status distinct from the Save (my save persists; the referent died)
- **Infographic (text-bearing raster)** — fit: `clean`; stress: Layout-aware OCR output (hOCR/ALTO) as a structure sidecar: representable as a signal, but worth stating explicitly; Tall-canvas thumbnailing is a UI problem, not a schema problem — correctly out of scope; Format identity lie (a '.gif' infographic that is actually webp): renditions carry declared type only — needs sniffed-format field (minor, shared with GIF)
- **Comic/webtoon episode + series** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: Access lifecycle {open | unlocked_until:date | paywalled | revoked} missing on Post/Save; Platform access models churn (Daily Pass killed within 5 years of launch) — access state must be data, not schema constants; Series containment + episode numbering blocked on the container gap
- **GIF (multi-format looping media + meme instance)** — fit: `fits_with_extension`; stress: No content hash (cryptographic or perceptual) on renditions → cross-platform dedup keyed only on permalink fails by construction; mediaKind photo-vs-video ambiguity for animated media (oEmbed providers disagree) — minor; Declared '.gif' vs actual codec: need sniffedFormat alongside declared mime — minor
- **RECURSIVE reference-capture (screenshot of tweet / photo of menu / photo of book page / re-shared post)** — fit: `gap`; stress: No depicts/capturesContent edge — the archetype's core relationship has no home; every workaround (references[] abuse, platformExtras, Concept-for-a-tweet) corrupts something else; REQUIRED-unique canonicalId+permalink outlaws phantom/provisional Posts — directly contradicts the census's own rule that nothing beyond identity + save-timestamp may be required; Resolution is a lifecycle: unresolved → candidate → resolved | offline | dead — no status field anywhere

Delisted products (census verified: Instagram deletes delisted products from users' saved lists), dead links, permanently-closed places, deleted wiki pages — the schema cannot distinguish 'alive at last check' from 'the platform record is gone but my capture survives', which is the product's core promise.
*Affects:* Product / e-commerce listing, Map / place listing, Bare link / unresolved reference, Canonical web document, Wiki / doc page
*Fix (folded into v3):* Add lifecycleState ∈ {live, changed, gated, delisted, dead} + lastVerifiedAt on Post. (Shared need with the ephemeral/live batch's state machine — design once.)

**[IMPORTANT]** User annotations/highlights have no home. Ebooks and PDFs are engaged over weeks with many highlights and reading positions per item; Save carries only sources/tags/savedAt/collections. For a product literally named after the commonplace book, the user's own marginalia is core data, not an afterthought.
*Affects:* Ebook, PDF / academic paper, Long-form article / newsletter, Canonical web document
*Fix (folded into v3):* Add an Annotation entity on Save that reuses the SAME evidence/selector machinery as extractions (W3C Web Annotation was designed for exactly this; Hypothes.is is the at-scale prior art). One selector system for machine extractions and human highlights — no parallel invention.

**[IMPORTANT]** No version/revision pin on Post — a save of a living artifact stores a moving pointer. A branch URL and a SHA-pinned permalink are indistinguishable; createdAt is ambiguous for versioned content.
*Affects:* code repo/file, living documents (wiki/Notion/Docs — other batch), long-form articles with edit drift
*Fix (folded into v3):* Optional Post.version {revisionId (commit SHA / revision number), versionPermalink, contentObservedAt} — distinct from createdAt (authorship) and savedAt (user relationship).

**[IMPORTANT]** No base-level interactive payload: poll/quiz options, per-option counts, close time, and viewer state exist on 7+ platforms but can only live in per-platform platformExtras — the same cross-platform structure lands in N incompatible namespaces, structurally killing cross-analysis (the product's core promise).
*Affects:* interactive poll/quiz, channel micro-posts with polls, community submissions with poll payloads
*Fix (folded into v3):* Lift AS2 Question into base (the schema already builds on AS2): interactive {kind ∈ {poll, quiz, form}, choiceMode: oneOf|anyOf, options[]{name, count}, closesAt, resultsAsOf, resultsFinal: bool, viewerState?{choice, result}}. Mastodon's replies.totalItems pattern is the reference serialization for counts.

**[IMPORTANT]** Save has no target selector — 'I saved slide 7', 'the July 22 occurrence', 'the blue/M variant', 'minutes 2:00–3:00' are all unrepresentable; the Save can only point at a whole Post.
*Affects:* multi-item container (child-level saves), event (occurrence of a recurring event), product (variant), time-range references (other batch)
*Fix (folded into v3):* Optional Save.targetSelector reusing the provenance selector types (text_quote/position, media_temporal, media_spatial) plus childIndex/childId and a variant slot — one mechanism for both evidence anchoring and save-scoping.

**[IMPORTANT]** renditions[] is AV/image-typed (Media RSS) — GPX tracks, ICS files, PDFs, USDZ/glTF bundles, file trees have no home; and renditions carry no content hash, so cross-platform content-level dedup (same GIF on Giphy and Tenor) fails by construction.
*Affects:* geo route (GPX), event (ICS), GIF (dedup), code repo (tree), runtime-dependent media & bounded rich files (other batches)
*Fix (folded into v3):* Generalize to assets[] {url|blobRef, declaredMime, sniffedFormat, role ∈ {rendition, attachment, sidecar, export}, contentHash, perceptualHash?}; renditions become assets with role:rendition, preserving Media RSS semantics for that role only.

**[IMPORTANT]** No lifecycle/access state on Post and no capture-fidelity record: delisted products (Instagram deletes them from users' saved lists — census-verified), Webtoon Ad Pass unlocks that expire after 3 days, and vote-gated poll results all produce saves whose referent is dead, locked, or partially blind — and the schema cannot say so.
*Affects:* product wishlist, comic/webtoon, interactive poll/quiz, ephemeral & live objects (other batches)
*Fix (folded into v3):* Post.lifecycle {status ∈ {live, changed, delisted, deleted, expired}, lastCheckedAt} + accessState {open, paywalled, unlocked_until: date, revoked} + captureFidelity {full, partial, metadata_only} with optional blind-region notes. Keep on Post (world-state), never on Save (user-state).

**[IMPORTANT]** StructuredContent slot values are bare — no observedAt timestamp — yet volatile typed attributes (price, availability, event date/venue, poll counts) are exactly what the user saved the item FOR; an undated price is future misinformation.
*Affects:* product wishlist, event, interactive poll/quiz, entity listings (other batch)
*Fix (folded into v3):* Slot values become {value, observedAt, source} observations; multiple observations per slot permitted (enables re-poll-at-close for polls and price history for products).

**[MINOR]** Live chat / audience co-produced content strains the signal model: a SIGNAL has one producer, but Twitch/Premiere chat (verified: chat replay persists on Premieres) is thousands of timestamped messages by distinct actors, co-present with the stream.
*Affects:* live stream, long video (Premiere chat replay)
*Fix (folded into v3):* Either allow a composite signal {channel: VERBAL_TEXT, producer: audience, messages[{actor, t, text}]} or model chat as intrinsic children — acceptable to defer, but decide before capturing any live content.

**[MINOR]** Compound local items (Live Photo = HEIC+MOV pair; multi-page scan = N images) strain renditions[]: Media RSS media:group is verified to mean 'the same content, yet different representations' (WAV vs MP3 of one song) — a still+video pair or page sequence is one logical item with MULTIPLE contents. Also media:hash is verified md5/sha-1 only — inadequate as the dedup identity hash.
*Affects:* Local photo, Scanned document, Local voice memo
*Fix (folded into v3):* Item-level urn:uuid identity + per-rendition role attribute (still|motion|page-N) + per-rendition RFC 6920 ni:sha-256 contentHash field (keep media:hash only as an export nicety).

**[MINOR]** body is a flat string; long-form articles and wiki pages are block-structured documents with inline media whose position is semantic. Census: 'needs a block model, not a string.'
*Affects:* Long-form article / newsletter, Wiki / doc page, Canonical web document
*Fix (folded into v3):* Cheapest sound path (defer the full block model): rendition = full HTML snapshot in renditions[]/rawPayloadRef; canonical extracted text = a VERBAL_TEXT signal that evidence anchors into (text_quote survives re-extraction better than text_position — document the drift risk). Revisit a block model only when annotation UX demands positional inline-media anchoring.

**[MINOR]** StructuredContent shell registry is a closed 4-enum (Recipe/HowTo/ItemList/ExercisePlan) — missing schema.org Event, Product, Menu, SoftwareSourceCode, Trip at minimum, and every new archetype reopens the schema.
*Affects:* event, product wishlist, recursive capture (photo of menu), code repo/file, geo route
*Fix (folded into v3):* Declare shells an open registry keyed to schema.org type URIs with the existing slot-filling contract (slots point down to entities/concepts); ship the 4 current + 5 named above as the seeded set.

**[MINOR]** Provenance selector registry is closed and has no geospatial selector — 'the climb at km 12 of this route' and map-region evidence have no legal anchor, while source_role IS extensible (inconsistent design).
*Affects:* geo route/list, entity listings with maps (other batch)
*Fix (folded into v3):* Make the selector registry extensible like source_role; seed geo_bounding (bbox/polygon) and track_offset (distance-along-track) selector types.

**[MINOR]** Extractions cannot locate a referent INSIDE a work: 'page 137 of this book' — grounding carries authority+externalId but no sub-work locator.
*Affects:* recursive capture (photo of book page), versioned works & bounded rich files (other batches)
*Fix (folded into v3):* Optional extraction.referentLocator {scheme ∈ {page, chapter, timestamp, CFI, line_range}, value} alongside grounding.

**[MINOR]** mediaKind {photo|video|rich|link} forces polls, routes, repos, 3D bundles, and mixed carousels into 'rich' — the field carries near-zero information for half of Batch C; mixed-media containers can't set it at all.
*Affects:* interactive poll/quiz, geo route, code repo/file, multi-item container, GIF (photo/video ambiguity)
*Fix (folded into v3):* Keep the oEmbed 4-enum for interop but add a secondary contentForm descriptor (open vocabulary: carousel, poll, route, source_code, model_3d, animation…) and permit mediaKind:null on containers whose kind lives in children.

**[MINOR]** Authority-policy inconsistency: Google Places (proprietary) sits inside the grounding spine while the policy mandates CC0/CC-BY; meanwhile OSM — the natural open geo authority — is ODbL and thus silently excluded. ASIN/GTIN are needed for products but absent from the enumerated authorities.
*Affects:* geo route/list, product wishlist, event (venues)
*Fix (folded into v3):* Add an explicit policy tier: 'identifier-only authorities' (Google Places, ASIN/GTIN, KYM) — IDs and links may be stored, content never ingested — distinct from the CC0/CC-BY ingested spine; admit OSM IDs under the same identifier-only tier.
