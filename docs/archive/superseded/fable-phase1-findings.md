### D2 — Pipeline spine: which document governs §7.A — the brief's six-stage pipeline or the repo's _ENGINE-groundup single-pass design
*Why:* Median clip is 29s — the whole clip fits one call. Multi-pass ensembles collapse inter-run agreement and poison the golden set; every always-on stage is solo-founder maintenance with no measured payoff. Reproducibility IS the moat. The brief and the repo's own newer doc disagree; if Phase 2 inherits the brief text uncritically it builds the over-heavy version.

### D3 — Grounding authority set: replace the ToS-broken KBs and fix where grounding executes
