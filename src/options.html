Places enrichment (optional)
Capture mode

Default (unchecked) is semi-auto: you open your TikTok saved page and click Sync; capture runs in your foreground tab while you're present.

Autonomous mode lets the extension open and drive a TikTok tab unattended. This puts the largest automated-scroll footprint on your real account and carries a higher rate-limit / account-safety risk. Capture is always paced to human cadence, but leave this off unless you want the click-once-and-forget workflow.
