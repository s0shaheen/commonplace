Commonplace — Settings

Bring your own keys. Everything here is stored locally in this browser (chrome.storage.local), never in the extension source. We can't read your library — that's the architecture, not a promise.

Capture

How the extension pulls your saved videos into the local library.

Mode

Default (off) is semi-auto: open your TikTok saved page and click Start capture — the run drives your foreground tab while you're present.

Autonomous mode lets the extension open and drive a TikTok tab unattended. It puts the largest automated-scroll footprint on your real account and carries a higher rate-limit / account-safety risk. Capture is always paced to a human cadence; leave this off unless you want the click-once-and-forget workflow.

Sources to capture

Unchecked sources are skipped this sweep. Favorites first is the recommended default.

Scroll speed

Normal is the tested cadence. Conservative scrolls more gently (safest for your session); Fast trades a little safety for speed.
